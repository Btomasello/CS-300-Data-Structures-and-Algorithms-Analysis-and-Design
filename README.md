# CS-300-Portfolio

*What was the problem you were solving in the projects for this course?
The main problem was that we needed to create a means of sorting various data while utilizing different models such as binary trees and hash tables. This data also needed to be readily accessible for the user to view and alter. 

*How did you approach the problem? Consider why data structures are important to understand.
I took a very linear approach due to how the course was structured. Each milestone was a new concept that I took the time to study and understand. When it came to tackling the problem, I thought about how each data structure had its own strengths and weaknesses depending on the situation. It was important to know how each of them were best utilized in order to best solve the problem at hand. Having this understanding made all the difference in creating the final product.

*How did you overcome any roadblocks you encountered while going through the activities or project?
Regarding the roadblocks I ran into, I mostly looked at youtube videos. Whenever I get stuck on anything, I always attempt to solve it myself first. This is usually the case unless I really mess up. For example, I always seem to mess up my workspaces in any software I tend to work with. When I started the course, I was messing around in VS Code and after I just could not find the typical panels for files, extensions, etc. Little did I know that I messed up the standard settings and only through looking online did I figure out how to fix it. It may seem silly, but we all have our weird weaknesses with technology.

*How has your work on this project expanded your approach to designing software and developing programs?
While working on this project, it really opened my eyes as to how much information is really out there. It's not about learning everything in one go, but learning, understanding, and being able to implement new concepts into your work. I feel like I can take on new material with a bit more confidence now. I have always had the issue of getting overwhelmed and trying to solve everything at once. During my work on this project or more specifically the milestones, I was able to let my brain process a bit at a time. I do also believe that my format for pseudocode really changed in the duration of this course. I believe it's at a bit of a higher standard than it was previously.

*How has your work on this project evolved the way you write programs that are maintainable, readable, and adaptable?
In terms of my standards alone, I know that I need to adhere to more strict guidelines to grow. Throughout this course, I may have completed assignments, but I know I could've have been more creative in my coding structure. I need to break away from the very cookie cutter standard that I have learned from tutorials and examples. I feel that while the way I write did evolve, it wasn't so much as growing so much as having an epiphine. I want to combine my base skills with a more abstract and creative form of thinking to bring my coding skills to the next level. This project helped open my eyes to really see my work from another perspective.
